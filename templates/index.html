About the model
Methodology
How to interpret this academic prototype

Risk dimensions

The dashboard displays deprivation, crime and antisocial behaviour, outlet density and health outcomes, alongside a composite index.

Application assessment

The adjusted data composite contributes 70% of the blended index; the mean of four officer ratings contributes 30%. Optional diversification conditions affect the adjusted data score.

0.70 × data + 0.30 × officer

Checker tiers: low below 38%, medium from 38% to below 60%, high from 60%.

Limitations

This is a coursework prototype using synthetic risk data. Scores are indices, not probabilities of harm. They are not validated for real licensing decisions. Unknown officer ratings currently default to zero; this may understate risk.

Composite dimension weights and data provenance should be documented against the backend implementation before real-world use.
